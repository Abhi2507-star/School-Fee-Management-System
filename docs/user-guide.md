# Staff Operations Manual (Administrator, Accountant, Cashier, Auditor)

## 1. Daily Cashier Counter Workflow
1. Sign in with your individual **Cashier** credentials at `http://localhost:3000`.
2. Open **Fee Collection (POS)** and enter the student's Admission Number or Name.
3. Review unpaid/partially paid installments, enter the received amount, select the payment mode (`CASH`, `UPI`, `BANK_TRANSFER`, `CHEQUE`, or `CARD`), and enter any reference number.
4. Click **Commit Payment & Issue Official Receipt**. Print the A4 or 80mm thermal receipt immediately.

## 2. Reprinting Historical Receipts
Open **Receipts & Reprints**, search by Receipt Number or Admission Number, and click **Reprint A4** or **80mm Thermal**. Reprinting uses the original receipt number and marks the document with `REPRINT COPY`.

## 3. Submitting & Approving Concessions, Reversals & Refunds
- **Accountants** submit reversal or refund requests with a mandatory reason.
- **Administrators** review and approve requests under **Concessions & Reversals** (maker-checker enforced).
